Does Screen Time Affect Students?

Will how many hours you sleep affect your anxiety?

How many hours you sleep has a direct effect on your anxiety because sleep loss impairs your brain's ability to regulate emotions. When you don't sleep enough, the connection between the logical part of your brain and its emotional center (the amygdala) weakens, causing you to overreact to daily stressors and perceive normal situations as threats. This sleep deprivation also spikes stress hormones like cortisol, keeping your body in a physical state of "fight-or-flight" by a racing heart and shallow breathing. Studies show that sleeping less than five hours puts you at the highest risk for severe anxiety spikes, while getting the recommended seven to nine hours provides the best emotional stability.

Is using tech at bedtime sacrifices the sleep students need to process and remember their lessons?

Yes it does, the blue light emitted by screens suppresses melatonin, making it harder to fall asleep and cutting directly into deep REM sleep. Because REM sleep is the critical phase where the brain processes daily learning and stores it into long-term memory, missing out on this deep rest actively leads to poorer memory retention and lower test scores the following day.

Does the total hours of rest a student achieves influence their stress levels?

Extensive academic and medical research shows that sleep duration is one of the most powerful predictors of a student's psychological and physiological stress. When students short-change their sleep, the connection between the logical part of their brain and its emotional center weakens, causing them to overreact to normal daily academic workloads.This ultimately traps students in a vicious cycle where a heavy academic workload causes them to sacrifice sleep, and the resulting exhaustion triggers a surge in anxiety that makes falling asleep the next night even harder.
